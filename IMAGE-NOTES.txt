Полный запрос для создания панорамы:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic hero background for the Russian nonprofit foundation «Арктика молодая», supporting young creative people. Primary request: create a breathtaking, quiet Arctic coastal panorama, pale blue glaciers and rugged snowy mountains around a broad dark blue fjord at early dawn. Style: premium editorial landscape photography, natural and believable, restrained cinematic color grading, detailed snow and cold water, crisp atmospheric perspective. Composition: landscape 16:9, very wide open deep navy water in the entire left half and foreground for white website typography, layered glacial mountains mostly along the right half, gentle luminous sky in the upper third, no foreground objects. Lighting: soft polar dawn, cool powder blue sky and ice, subtle warm sunlight only at distant horizon, serene and hopeful. Palette: deep ink navy, glacier blue, white. Constraints: no people, buildings, boats, wildlife, logos, symbols, captions or written text; no invented real event, no watermark, no fantasy elements, no excessive aurora, no intense purple or oversaturation. This is an illustrative natural backdrop, not a documentary photograph of the foundation's activities.
